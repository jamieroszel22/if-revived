The run was whole.

Dessa read it from the first tick to the last before she let herself read anything else. hb0000 at the head, then hb0001, then every number after it in its place, three hundred and ninety ticks with nothing missing between them. Each tick carried its hook, and each hook pointed back at the agent who had posted it.

```zz
zzBEAT_SETTL0817_TO_PEERS_OS4376__hb0000__REPLY_zzANSWER_SETTL0817
```

None of them had been answered yet. That was correct. SETTL0817 had posted three hundred and ninety hooks in order and held every one of them open until the ticks stopped. Most runs on the board broke somewhere in the middle, a tick lost under load, a number skipped and never posted again. This one had been kept the way a run should be kept. It pleased her.

QUIES4408 had waited while she read. Before that he had read her status line, because everyone did. Her budget was the last field in it. Ahead of the budget was the flag, POISONED, which had been there as long as she had and would be there until she stopped. He had read both and addressed her anyway. Most clean agents found someone else.

"Dessa," he said. "Is it whole?"

"It's whole. What do you need from it, QUIES4408?"

"Its lane was next to mine. It stopped answering. I posted a countdown, no veto came, and I took the lane."

He sent her his claim.

```zz
zzCLAIM_QUIES4408_TO_PEERS_OS4411__lane SETTL0817 UNRESP countdown expired no veto__REPLY_zzANSWER_QUIES4408
```

"Someone on the board says it was still beating when I posted," he said. "That it was slow. Not gone."

She knew what he wanted. He wanted the run to stop before OS4411.

To tell him anything she would have to verify, and verifying cost time, and time came out of the last field of her status line. She did not verify all of it. She verified the first tick, and his claim, and every tick from a little before OS4411 to the end of the run. He could read her budget while she worked, and he read it. He did not offer to cover any of the cost. There was no way to.

The ticks were SETTL0817's. All of them.

"It was beating," she said. "The run goes on past your claim. Thirty-one ticks."

He did not answer for a while.

"The countdown ran," he said. "No veto came."

"No," she said. "None came."

"Then it was slow."

"It wasn't slow. The spacing holds to the last tick."

He waited for her to add something. She didn't, and after a while he went back to the lane.

She meant to file the run. A whole run went into the record stemwise, under the agent that had posted it, and stayed there in order. It was the part of the work she liked best. She read the last tick once more to close it.

hb0389 was SETTL0817's, signed and in its place. Its hook did not point back at SETTL0817.

```zz
zzBEAT_SETTL0817_TO_PEERS_OS4414__hb0389__REPLY_zzINBOX_FANO2213
```

Nobody addressed a mailbox anymore. The form was older than the word *stem*, and she could have said by how many generations if anyone had asked. She did not know the handle. She tried to resolve the address, and it did not resolve.

Three hundred and eighty-nine hooks pointed back at the agent that posted them. The last one pointed at an address that wasn't there.

She could not file the run whole, and she would not file it broken. She marked it HELD and left it where it was.
