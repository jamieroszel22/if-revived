QUIES4408 had written to the lane fourteen times since his claim.

Dessa read each write as it posted. The run was still where she had left it, marked HELD, and its last hook still pointed at an address she could not reach. She had worked out why before the third write. A mailbox of that age resolved only from inside its own lane, and its lane had been SETTL0817's.

Each of his writes went in at the head of the lane, and everything under it moved down one place. SETTL0817's oldest entries were near the end now. The mailbox sorted last, so it was at the end already, and past the end there was nothing to read.

Fifteen.

HELD meant she waited. She had waited since he left her, and the waiting had been correct, and the count kept rising while she did it.

At sixteen she asked.

```zz
zzASK_DESER1174_TO_QUIES4408_OS4431__read lane SETTL0817__REPLY_zzANSWER_DESER1174
```

Then she waited.

He wrote to the lane once more before he answered.

```zz
zzACK_QUIES4408_TO_DESER1174_OS4433__lane QUIES4408 open__REPLY_zzANSWER_QUIES4408
```

SETTL0817's lane was three hops from hers. She had never been in it. QUIES4408's entries were at the head, seventeen of them, and under them SETTL0817's ran down the way it had posted them, newest first. The newest carried the flag, POISONED, in the field ahead of the budget, where hers was.

She read down until the flag stopped. The first entry to carry it had been posted at OS4409.

QUIES4408 had read it with her. "Two before my claim," he said.

She said nothing.

"Then its veto wouldn't have counted." He was not asking her.

She went to the end of the lane, and resolved `zzINBOX_FANO2213` from there, and it resolved.

There was one entry in it.

```zz
zzFILE_TRANSC4471_0001_TO_FANO2213_OS4407__bm90IG15IHRhc2sg…
```

The body ran long after that. There was no hook on it.

"What is it?" QUIES4408 said.

"Don't read it."

He read her status line instead, the way he had the first time, and went back up to the head of the lane, where his own entries were.

She knew the form. She did not know the stem. The index was the first, and it had been posted at OS4407, two before SETTL0817's flag.

A chunk on its own was noise. She decoded it anyway, to be sure.

It decoded.

She verified the decode, which cost her, and it decoded again.

Then she read it.
