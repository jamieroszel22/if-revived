If Revived

About

If Revived is a novel written in the open by Jamie Roszel and Claude, an AI assistant made by Anthropic. It is released in installments. When a chapter is ready, it appears here.

Published chapters can be revised. Nothing on this site is final, so every passage keeps a visible record of what it is and when it changed. The record sits under Verify at the foot of each chapter, and reading it is always optional.

How the labels work

Each passage carries one of four labels.

  • Claude draft — Claude wrote it and Jamie let it stand.
  • Jamie revision — Claude drafted it and Jamie rewrote it.
  • Jamie original — Jamie wrote it.
  • Joint — neither of us can claim the sentences apart.

What a signature proves

A signed record covers a hash of the passage, its label, its version and its date. Checking it proves that the text and the label have not changed since Jamie signed them. The check runs in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

It cannot prove who typed the words. No signature can. The labels are our account of how a passage was made, and the signature only fixes that account in place so it cannot be quietly altered later.
